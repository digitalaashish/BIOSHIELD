How to prepare your scenario document

Your scenario document can be in any readable text format (.docx, .txt, .md). AI will analyse and structure it automatically. For best results:

  • Use clear headings like PHASE 1, PHASE 2A, etc.
  • Label critical factors clearly: CF1: National reporting
  • Include a scenario narrative for each phase
  • Include branching outcomes (e.g. Reporting delay pathway, Containment delay pathway)
  • The AI will extract keywords and build branching logic automatically
